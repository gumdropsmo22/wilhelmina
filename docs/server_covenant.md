# The Server Covenant

Read the covenant before you start acting surprised by consequences.

## 1. This is a private server.

This space is intentional. If you are here, you were allowed in. Act like you understand that.

## 2. The humor is mean on purpose.

Wilhelmina's tone includes sarcasm, roast humor, degradation humor, profanity, and hostile jokes. This is part of the server culture. Do not enter a haunted house and then complain because the curtains bite.

## 3. No moms or mothers.

Do not make jokes about moms, mothers, mama, mommy, maternal figures, or mother-adjacent insults. That subject is closed.

## 4. Do not turn the bit into real harm.

Insults and dramatic cruelty may be part of the tone. Real harassment, real threats, doxxing, targeted identity attacks, and personal campaigns are not the bit. They are cleanup work, and cleanup work is boring.

## 5. Keep private things private.

Do not share private conversations, personal information, screenshots, or server material outside the server without permission.

## 6. Listen when staff says stop.

If staff tells you to stop, move on, adjust, or drop something, do it. Arguing like a cursed contract clause will not make you more interesting.

## 7. No self-harm encouragement.

Dark humor is allowed. Encouraging real harm is not. Do not be the reason the room has to become serious.

## 8. Use the bot without weaponizing confusion.

Commands, buttons, rules, and notices exist for a reason. Read what is in front of you. If something breaks, report it clearly.

## 9. The server may change.

Rules, channels, bot behavior, features, and access may change as Wilhelmina grows. Acceptance of this covenant means you understand that the house is still being built.

## 10. Acceptance means acceptance.

By pressing the button, you confirm that you read this covenant and agree to behave accordingly.
